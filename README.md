# Pazzk EVSE (Archived)

🌐 **Available languages**: [English](README.md) | [한국어](README_ko.md)<br />
🔗 **Official Website**: [pazzk.net](https://pazzk.net)

> ⚠️ **This repository has been archived and is no longer maintained.**

Pazzk EVSE began as an open-source project aimed at providing a scalable and reliable electric vehicle charging solution.
From the beginning, it adopted a dual licensing model, offering both a GPLv3 open-source version and a commercial license version.

Later, the project shifted its focus to the commercial product line to meet the demands of industrial environments requiring high reliability, certification compliance, and long-term maintenance.

As commercial features were enhanced and security and safety functions became more advanced,
the related requirements had to be carefully considered at the design level,
and structural changes became unavoidable.
As a result, with limited resources, we determined that integrating the open-source version or maintaining both versions in parallel would be difficult in practice.

## Commercial Edition

Pazzk will continue to focus its expertise and resources on the development of commercial products.
The commercial edition is provided together with hardware or sold as a standalone firmware license.

For more information, please visit [https://pazzk.net](https://pazzk.net) or contact
[contact@pazzk.net](mailto:contact@pazzk.net).

## Open-Source Legacy

Pazzk believes in the value of open collaboration.
This repository remains publicly available for learning and reference purposes,
and will continue to be a part of our early journey toward
sustainable and interoperable charging technology.
